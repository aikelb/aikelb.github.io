When I started out in specialty coffee I kept rewriting the same pour-over math on scraps of paper, so I built this little calculator. Set your dose and ratio, pick a method, and it lays out every pour with timings. There's a timer too, for when both hands are busy.

The recipes come from people who know far more than I do: the Tetsu Kasuya 4-6 breakdown and the Hoffmann tests are from [Firefortysix](https://firefortysix.com/2021/08/21/putting-the-flexibility-of-tetsu-4-6-to-the-test/), the Ultimate V60 and Switch methods from [James Hoffmann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QjIvN8mlK9Y), and the iced recipe from [Hueguh](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y3jxJv5UiPg).
