For a decade, from 2014 to 2024, I mentored at the annual **Island Jam** in Gran Canaria, a local game jam built on the Ludum Dare format. It brings students, hobbyists, and professionals together for a weekend of making something from nothing. As a mentor I mostly help teams with game design and with their code: tracking down bugs, untangling architecture, getting a prototype back on its feet before the deadline.

I also taught Unity3D courses through **ACADEVI**, the Canary Islands game developer association, back when the local scene was still small and we were all figuring it out together.

Game jams are still the fastest way I know to learn. You ship something in 48 hours, work with people you met that morning, and find out what you can actually do under pressure. I've watched complete beginners go from "I don't know how to code" to a playable prototype in a single weekend, and that never gets old.
