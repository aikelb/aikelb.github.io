I live in the Canary Islands with my partner and our two cats, Marvel and Haru. There's usually a pourover somewhere nearby. I've been working remotely since 2011, back when you had to explain what that meant at parties.

<div class="section-header" style="margin-top: var(--space-xl);">

I build AI systems at **INNO-VERSE**, mostly Python, LangChain, and FastAPI. Before that I spent fourteen years making games. Shipped titles for Steam, Meta Quest, Nintendo Switch, and iOS at PlayMedusa. Ran a micro-studio called Aurita Games that somehow accumulated over 80 projects. Built VR multiplayer systems at 30 Parallel. It's been a varied path.

I also teach, or at least I used to. Lectured on game programming at the **University of Las Palmas** from 2014 to 2020, delivering a year-long course in game design and programming using Unity3D and C#. Mentored at the **Exel Gaming Accelerator** in Riyadh. Helped organize community game jams. Also run game development workshops for vocational training students. Earned a **Master's in Teacher Training** for Secondary Education in 2025, with a research study on using videogame development as an educational tool for STEM.
