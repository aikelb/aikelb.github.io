I live in the Canary Islands with my partner, our two cats (Marvel and Haru), and a steady supply of single-origin filter coffee. I've been working remotely since 2011 — long before it was fashionable.

## What I do

I build AI agents at **INNO-VERSE** — mostly Python, LangChain, and FastAPI. Before that, I spent fourteen years making games. I shipped titles for Steam, Meta Quest, Nintendo Switch, and iOS at PlayMedusa, led a small studio called Aurita Games (80+ projects), and built VR multiplayer systems at 30 Parallel.

I also teach. From 2014 to 2020 I lectured on game programming at the **University of Las Palmas**. I've mentored at the **Exel Gaming Accelerator** in Riyadh and helped organize community game jams. I hold a Master's in Teacher Training for Secondary Education.
