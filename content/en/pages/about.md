I live in the Canary Islands. My partner and I are expecting our first child, and I’m in love with the idea of becoming a family. My two cats, Marvel and Haru, are the best in the world (you can even put up a Christmas tree and they leave it alone). There’s usually a pourover somewhere nearby. I’ve been working remotely since 2011, back when you had to explain what that meant at parties.

<div class="section-header">
  <span class="section-index">[01 &middot; What I do]</span>
</div>

These days, I’m focused on building AI systems at INNO-VERSE, mostly using Python, LangChain, and FastAPI. My background, however, is rooted in game development. For fourteen years, my coworker and I built games at **PlayMedusa** while also running our own micro-studio, **Aurita Games**. We operated across both brands, sharing the same portfolio and shipping more than 80 projects for Steam, Meta Quest, Nintendo Switch, iOS, and web. It’s been a varied path.

I also **teach**. Lectured on game programming at the University of Las Palmas from 2014 to 2020, delivering a year-long course in game design and programming using Unity3D and C#. **Mentored** at the Exel Gaming Accelerator in Riyadh. Helped organize community game jams and ran game development workshops for vocational training students. Earned a **Master's in Teacher Training** for Secondary Education in 2025, with a research study on using videogame development as an educational tool for STEM.
