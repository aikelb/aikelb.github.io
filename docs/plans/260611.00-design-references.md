# Design references

Pinned references for all design work on this site. The goal: a modern portfolio that is
appealing to all publics, even though most content is developer-oriented. Keep the current
vibe — humble and authentic — while raising the visual and motion quality.

## leanrada.com — personality & original design

What we like:

- Strong personal personality; the site feels handmade, not templated.
- Original card design: hover states with padding/layout shifts, playful micro-interactions.
- Animations that feel intentional and tied to content, not decorative noise.

See also the earlier deep-dive: [250715.01-leanrada-analysis.md](250715.01-leanrada-analysis.md).

## mise.jdx.dev — clean & colorful

What we like:

- Very clean layout that still feels colorful and warm; appealing to the eye without clutter.
- Good example of a developer-tool site that reads well for non-developers.
- Confident use of accent color against a calm base palette.

## reactbits.dev — motion quality

What we like:

- Smooth, elegant canvas animations (backgrounds, particles) that stay subtle.
- Hover and looping animations on bento boxes and components; motion is fluid and never janky.
- Component-level animation polish: easing, duration, and restraint.

Note: this site is plain 11ty + vanilla JS (Alpine.js available) — reactbits patterns are
inspiration to re-derive with CSS/canvas/vanilla JS, not React components to import.

## Constraints that apply to all of the above

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any canvas/looping animation.
- Keep the site fast: no heavy animation libraries; prefer CSS, small vanilla JS, or a single
  lightweight canvas.
- Tone stays humble and authentic; design adds warmth and craft, not flash for its own sake.
